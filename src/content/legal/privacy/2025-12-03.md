# Privacy Policy

Effective: 23.06.23

## 1. Introduction

At SupportMail, we take the protection of your privacy seriously. We do not collect any personalized data. Any information you provide while using our services may be used to improve our products and services. However, this is not done automatically, and your personal data will not be shared with third parties.

---

## 2. What data do we collect?

Overall, the following information is stored in our database:

- User IDs
- Role IDs
- Guild IDs
- Message IDs
- Channel ID
- Guilds icon hashes
- Guild names
- Timestamps
- Access tokens (encrypted)
- Every other data that you provide (when interaction with the bot which means executing commands, sending direct messages or messages in ticket posts)

### 2.1. Use of the access token

The access token is used to determine the mutual guilds between the user and the bot as this is also the most efficient way to check users' permissions on a specific server.

### 2.2. Use of collected data

We use all collected data to provide our services and improve them.

We use the information to personalize and improve your user experience.

We use the data to manage tickets and reports.

We also access the stored data and Discord's data in order to provide support like stated in the next section.

### 2.3. Temporary Data Retrieval

We (the owner of the App, as well as the manager and the moderators of the support server) may retrieve certain information about you and your interactions with the App, including but not limited to:

- User data (e.g., username, user ID, and avatar)\
  Please note that we store your access token securely, which is generated when you log in with Discord, in an encrypted format. When we retrieve your data, we only see whether or not you have an access token set.
- Guild data and configuration (e.g., guild name, guild ID, and also any configurations like the ticket forum and the tickets)

This information is only accessible to the bot owners and, in limited cases, to moderators of our support server.

If you want to know more about this and see an example, kindly ask in the [support server](https://discord.gg/rFkVQx3vpj).

### 2.4. Appeal System Data

When you submit an appeal through our appeal system, we collect and store the following information:

- Appeal form responses, including explanations, context, and any statements you provide
- Contact information (email address, Discord username and ID)
- Supporting evidence you voluntarily upload (screenshots, logs, or other files)
- Appeal timestamps and submission metadata
- Confirmations provided during the appeal process

This data is used solely for:

- Processing and reviewing your appeal
- Investigating the circumstances of your restriction or ban
- Maintaining records for security and abuse prevention
- Improving our moderation and appeal processes

Appeal data is accessible only to authorized SupportMail staff and relevant moderators involved in the review process. We retain appeal data for a minimum of 90 days after resolution to maintain proper records, and may retain it longer for cases involving serious violations or security concerns.

If your appeal is successful, appeal data may be retained to monitor compliance with any conditions of reinstatement. If you withdraw your appeal or it is denied, the appeal data will be retained according to our stand.

You may request deletion of your appeal data by contacting us directly (through a ticket on the support server or via [email](#9-contact)), though we reserve the right to retain certain information for security and legal compliance purposes.

## 3. Security

All servers and databases used to provide our services are not available to the public. Some information may be available through designated endpoints but only with proper authorization.

It should be noted, that we are doing our best to protect your information, but no method of electronic transmission or storage is 100% secure and no one can guarantee absolute data security. We are not liable for any data loss or data breach.

For transparency, when a configuration is reset - if it wasn't initiated by the server owner - the bot sends the details to the owner. This includes the username, user ID, and avatar of the user who performed the reset, along with detailed information about what was reset.

### 3.1. Cloudflare Managed Challenge (Dashboard)

The dashboard is protected by Cloudflare's Bot Management ("Managed Challenge") to detect and block bots and other automated or abusive traffic before it reaches our infrastructure.

This processing is carried out by Cloudflare in accordance with its own [Privacy Policy](https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/). We only receive aggregated data from Cloudflare about how many requests were allowed or blocked — we do not receive any personal data collected by Cloudflare as part of this service.

## 5. Use of Message Content Intent

The bot utilizes this intent to read messages within the ticket forum. This is achieved by utilising the /setup tickets command. Please note that the bot will not view any other message content within a guild. This is because the function will be cancelled if the channel ID does not match a ticket post ID in the database.

The bot does not retain any message content retrieved by this intent, and only accesses and processes it during two processes.

The message ID, post ID, and server ID are stored in the database until the ticket is closed.

The bot edits its own messages (in both DMs and ticket posts) based on the new message contents and attachments. In the event that a message is edited and the channel ID matches a post ID from the database, and the ticket is still open, the bot will attempt to edit its message in the user's DM to align with the new content and new attachments in the ticket post.

## 6. Duration

### 6.1. Guild data

Guild data is stored for the duration of the bot's presence within the guild. Once the bot is removed from the guild, the data will be stored for an additional seven days for reasons related to user experience.

Should a user wish to have the data of their guild deleted immediately, they are required to create a ticket in the support server (ID: 1064594649668395128), including their guild ID, and proof of ownership of the guild. Please note that this process may take up to 24 hours, depending on the time the ticket is submitted. While we will try to process your request within 24 hours, please note that we cannot guarantee a reply within that same timeframe.

### 6.2. User data

The access token will expire after seven days, but it may be stored for a longer period. Please be advised that if no updates are made to your data for a period of one year, it will be deleted.

Should you wish to request the immediate deletion of your user data, please send a direct message to LukeZ (ID: 506893652266844162) on Discord.

Please be advised that any direct messages you send to the bot will result in the collection of new data.

### 6.3. Subscription data

Please note that subscription data will not be deleted automatically and cannot be manually deleted. This is done for security and legal compliance reasons.

### 6.4. Log data and backups

Note that data deletion may not apply to log data and backups.
